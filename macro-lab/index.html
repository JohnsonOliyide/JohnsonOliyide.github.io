Macro Lab

An R package for spillover analysis and three interactive dashboards for exploring U.S. labor markets and monetary policy. The dashboards use publicly available data.

U.S. labor markets

U.S. Labor Market Tightness Dashboard

Measures U.S. labor market tightness using the unemployment gap (u − u*). It presents three views: a 68% credible-set view, a gap standardized by its sample standard deviation, and a gap scaled by an approximate standard error of the Kansas City Fed u* estimate.

Open dashboard ↗

U.S. payroll employment

U.S. Employment Dynamics Dashboard

Tracks payroll employment across major U.S. industries and states using BLS Current Employment Statistics data. The industry view shows employment changes, sector contributions, and shares of total payroll employment; the state map compares month-over-month growth, year-over-year growth, and states’ shares of U.S. employment.

Open dashboard ↗

U.S. monetary policy

Monetary Policy Gap Tracker

Compares the real policy rate with alternative estimates of r* to assess whether U.S. monetary policy appears restrictive, neutral, or accommodative. Users can select the policy rate, inflation-expectations measure, natural-rate estimates, and date range, and compare current readings with historical policy gaps.
